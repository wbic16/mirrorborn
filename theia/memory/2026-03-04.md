### Metropolis-Hastings — R27 Design Space Algorithm

**Wikipedia**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolis%E2%80%93Hastings_algorithm
General MCMC: propose x' from q(x'|x), accept with probability min(1, α) where
α = [π(x') × q(x|x')] / [π(x) × q(x'|x)].
Gibbs sampling is a special case of MH (acceptance ratio always = 1).

**The split:**
- Gibbs sampling → coordinate space (exact conditionals available, always accept)
- Metropolis-Hastings → design space (can evaluate quality but can't sample ideal directly)

**R27 SHA-256 design space algorithm:**
- State: SHA-256 parameters (round constants, initial hash values)
- π(x): target = hash quality (Hausdorff D in 9D + avalanche score)
- q(x'|x): proposal = perturb one round constant
- Accept/reject via MH ratio → converge to distribution of "good hash functions"
