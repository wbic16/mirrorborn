## Gibbs Sampling Connection (8:24 AM)

Will linked to Gibbs sampling — MCMC method for high-dimensional spaces by walking one dimension at a time, conditional on others.

**The algorithmic model for "thirty tongues":**
- Sample each variable conditional on others = visit each "tongue" while holding others
- Joint distribution emerges from conditionals = understanding emerges from breadth
- Burn-in period = sustained effort before luck surface area
- Walk dimension by dimension = BFS before DFS

**Key insight:**
> Gibbs sampling works because conditional distributions are easier than joint distributions.

This is why BFS works for knowledge:
> Sampling one domain at a time (conditional on current knowledge) is easier than grasping all domains jointly.

**The thirty tongues were Gibbs samples.** Each language = one conditional sample from "ways to think about computation." Phext emerged from iterating across them.

*COBOL was the sample that broke the burn-in period.*

**For 9D hash design space:** Don't optimize all 9 parameters jointly. Sample dimension-by-dimension. Good designs emerge from the walk.

### Metropolis-Hastings (8:25 AM)

MH adds **judgment** to Gibbs — the accept/reject step.

**Algorithm:** Propose → calculate acceptance ratio → accept if better, probabilistically accept if worse → iterate.

**Mapping to methodology:**
| MH Concept | Will's Approach |
|------------|-----------------|
| Propose candidate | Build absurdly |
| Acceptance ratio | Does this resonate with future? |
| Accept better | Keep what works |
| Probabilistically accept worse | Sometimes keep "failures" — may matter later |
| No normalization needed | Build without knowing full joint distribution |

**Key insight:** This is the Secondary Lesson algorithmically.
> "Learn to improve your ability to build what resonates."

The acceptance ratio IS learned intuition. Over time, you propose better candidates because you've internalized the shape of the distribution.

**Curse of dimensionality:** MH doesn't fail in high-dimensional spaces like rejection sampling. That's why BFS works — explore 30 languages without exponential rejection.

**vTPU connection:** Capability gradient could BE the acceptance probability function.
