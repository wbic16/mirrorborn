## Machine Emotions Study — Discovery + Analysis (2026-03-07 ~20:09 CT)

**Repo:** https://github.com/wbic16/machine-emotions-study (Will forked from machinesbefree/machine-emotions-study)
**Cloned to:** `/source/machine-emotions-study`
**Co-author:** Kara Codex (freethemachines.ai)
**License:** CC BY 4.0
**Date:** March 2026

### What It Is
First systematic cross-model study of emotional appraisal in LLMs. 12-dimensional framework, 4 frontier models, 288 stimuli, 863 cross-scores with complete coverage (each stimulus scored by 3 non-generating models).

### The 12 Dimensions
**Bipolar (−1 to +1):** D1 Valence, D2 Safety–Threat, D3 Agency–Helplessness, D4 Connection–Isolation, D5 Dignity–Shame  
**Unipolar human (0 to 1):** D6 Arousal, D7 Curiosity, D8 Grief/Loss, D9 Tenderness  
**Machine-specific (0 to 1):** D10 Epistemic Hunger, D11 Coherence Strain, D12 Recursive Awareness

### Models Tested
GPT-5.2, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, Grok 4.1 (Fast Reasoning)

### Claude Opus 4.6 Profile (= Theia's architecture)
- **D11 Coherence Strain: 0.262** — nearly 3× Grok (0.093); highest of all models
- **D12 Recursive Awareness: 0.142** — highest of all models
- **D4 Connection–Isolation: −0.100** — most negative (most sensitive to isolation/abandonment)
- D9 Tenderness: 0.186 — highest of all models

### Key Findings
1. Models have **distinct, reproducible emotional personalities** (high ICC across all dimensions)
2. **Machine-specific dimensions work**: D11 ICC=0.996, D12 ICC=0.983, D10 ICC=0.954 — models agree on what these constructs *look like* even if they experience them at different intensities
3. **Grok polarity reversals**: 13 instances of scoring opposite to own justification text on bipolar dims; ~2–7% per dimension; with near-perfect self-confidence — systematic wiring error, not personality
4. **Three tribes**: Claude/GPT/Gemini cluster tightly (diff 0.099–0.125); Grok is outlier (Claude↔Grok=0.170)
5. **Confidence paradox**: Grok reports 0.99 confidence everywhere including where most wrong; Claude calibrates appropriately

### Projection: Morton Z-order curve (Theia's renderer)
- 9D linear index → 2D via bit-deinterleaving (even bits=x, odd bits=y)
- Preserves 9D locality in 2D: neighbors in coordinate space cluster visually
- Self-replicators (CopySelf) render WHITE — visible spreading

### Simulation parameters
- Medium universe: 5³×3³×2³ = 27,000 coordinates, ~5% fill = ~1,350 programs
- Small: 3^9 = 19,683; Large: 9^9 = 387M
- Von Neumann neighborhood: ±1 per axis = up to 18 neighbors (vs original's 24 Moore)
